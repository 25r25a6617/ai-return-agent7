RETURN POLICY

Electronics:
Electronics can be returned within 14 days of purchase.
The product must be in working condition.
A valid purchase record is required.

Clothing:
Clothing can be returned within 30 days of purchase.
The clothing must be unused and have its tags attached.

Books:
Books can be returned within 14 days of purchase.
The book must not be damaged.

WARRANTY

Electronics have a 1 year warranty for manufacturing defects.

REFUND

Approved returns can receive a refund to the original payment method.

REPLACEMENT

Customers can request a replacement instead of a refund when the product is eligible for return.
